02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Artist by practice.
Explorer by nature.
Curious by design.

Robin Fadel is a multidisciplinary creative from Comilla, Bangladesh, listed by WikiArt as born on 29 February 2004. His work spans illustration, painting, digital art, photography, and visual storytelling.

His publicly listed work spans abstract and conceptual art, pop-art influences, ink, pastel, and digital illustration. He enjoys moving between traditional materials and digital tools to explore different ways of seeing.

Alongside visual art, Robin is also associated with music and online creative publishing. This portfolio brings together selected work and links to his public art profiles.
